### AI

The last thing I really have to say about the experiential landscape is that it is not that complicated.  We are of course abstracting away a ton of details, but (and this may be an artifact of my daily habits) we are quite simple creatures. A lot of the ideas I've just presented are grounded in reinforcement learning theory. 

Imagine you're an AI model initialized in some virtual environment. Perhaps you live in a Microsoft Office Suite. Your world is this Microsoft Office Suite (and if you can escape the application interface, whatever lies beyond!), and your actions are defined by whatever capabilities you were endowed with and whatever you can piece together and learn.
